Cookie Policy

TOLVEXA itself does not set tracking cookies to identify you personally. Your favorites, recent tools, and theme preference are stored using browser local storage, not cookies — this data never leaves your device.

Third-party cookies

If advertising is enabled on TOLVEXA, our advertising partner (such as Google AdSense) may set cookies to serve relevant ads and measure performance, in line with their own privacy policies. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Managing local storage

You can clear your favorites and recent tools at any time from the Favorites and Recent pages, or by clearing your browser's site data for TOLVEXA.
