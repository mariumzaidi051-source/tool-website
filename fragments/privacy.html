Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

TOLVEXA is designed to collect as little information about you as possible. This page explains what does and doesn't happen with your data.

No account required

TOLVEXA does not have user accounts or sign-in. Every visitor uses TOLVEXA as a guest. Nothing you enter into a tool is tied to an identity we control.

Local storage, not our servers

Your favorite tools, recently used tools, and theme preference (dark or light mode) are saved using your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device and is never transmitted to TOLVEXA or any third party. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

Tool processing

Wherever technically practical, TOLVEXA tools process your input — text, files, or images — entirely in your browser. That data is not uploaded to a server. If a specific tool ever needs to send data to a server to function, that will be stated clearly on that tool's page.

Analytics and advertising

TOLVEXA may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand overall traffic patterns, and may display advertisements in clearly marked ad spaces. Advertising partners, if enabled, may use cookies or similar technology as described in our Cookie Policy.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email address and message you provide are sent via your own email client to reach us — TOLVEXA does not store contact form submissions on a server.

Children's privacy

TOLVEXA is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TOLVEXA evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent through our Contact page.
